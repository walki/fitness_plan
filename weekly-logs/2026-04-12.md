### Tuesday 3x10 Sweet Spot — Zwift Custom Workout

**FTP: 217W**

```
Warmup:        10:00  —  Zone 1–2 ramp, 120W → 160W
Activation 1:   1:00  —  175W (80% FTP) — wake the legs up
Recovery:        1:00  —  130W
Activation 2:   1:00  —  190W (88% FTP) — feel what sweet spot is today
Recovery:        2:00  —  130W

Interval 1:    10:00  —  173–195W (80–90% FTP), target 184W mid-range
                         Cadence 85–95. Settle in, find a rhythm.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W — spin easy, don't stop pedaling

Interval 2:    10:00  —  173–195W, target 184W
                         If interval 1 felt easy, nudge toward 190W.
                         If it felt hard, stay at 178–180W. No ego.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W

Interval 3:    10:00  —  173–195W, target 184W
                         Last one. If you've got more, push to 190–195W
                         in the final 3 minutes. If not, just hold steady.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W

Cooldown:        5:00  —  140W → 100W ramp down
```

**Total time: ~65 min** (including warm-up and cooldown)
**Time at sweet spot: 30 min**

**Execution notes:**
- This is a reintroduction, not a test. If 173W feels hard after 5 minutes, drop to 165W and finish the interval. Completing 3x10 at a moderate effort beats blowing up at 195W after 2x7.
- Watch HR — it'll drift through each interval. If HR is climbing above 155 by mid-interval 2, you're going too hard. Back off 5–10W.
- Hip check: if the crash hip is affecting your pedal stroke or position, cut it to 2x10 and call it a win.
- Cadence target 85–95 keeps it aerobic. Don't grind at 75.

**Sunday Long Ride — Grail Outdoors**

- Duration: 3–3.5 hrs
- Effort: Mixed — mostly Z2 (140–155W) but include some rolling terrain effort. Don't shy away from pushing over climbs, but don't go hunting for intensity.
- This is the last big training day before Florida. Ride somewhere fun. Simulate a loaded feel if the terrain allows.
- Fuel properly: aim for 60–70g carbs/hr from the start. Practice what you should have done at Mo Gritty.
- **The day before (Saturday):** eat 2,500+ cal, 300+ g carbs. This is the pre-load dry run for Black Fork.
