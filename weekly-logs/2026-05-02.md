### Race narrative
- Weather: 40–45°F, some hail, light rain. Cold but not a problem except glasses fogging — went squinty-eyed for last 10 miles on descents.
- Pacing: Much better discipline early. Didn't chase the opening surge. Built into the race.
- Fueling: Pre-loaded properly the day before. Good breakfast race morning. 1,200 cal on-bike.
- Finish: Strong and feeling good. Night and day vs Mo Gritty.
- Elapsed vs moving: Only 14 min of stops vs nearly 2 hours at Mo Gritty. No naps, no extended walking breaks.

### What the build delivered
Every piece of the 5-week Black Fork build showed up on race day:
1. **Pre-load protocol** — learned from Mo Gritty bonk, executed correctly
2. **Pacing discipline** — didn't blow up early, finished strong
3. **Aerobic base** — 178W NP at 134 HR for 5 hours. Deep engine.
4. **Florida volume** — 282 miles of loaded riding primed the aerobic system
5. **Mental confidence** — knew the terrain from Mo Gritty, trusted the process

### FTP implications
NP 178W for 5 hours at HR 134 with IF 0.822 (based on 217W FTP). The stale FTP inflates the IF — at a real FTP of ~240W, this would be IF 0.74, which is right where a 5-hour gravel race should be. Still need to validate post-race.
