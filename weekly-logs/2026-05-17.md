## 🏁 Red Eagle Plan (Revised Thu May 14 — Train Through It)

**Course:** 42.6 mi, 1,479 ft climbing. Flatter and faster than Black Fork.
**New framing:** Not a target race. Hard 3-hr Sunday ride in race kit with a number plate. No beer, lots of effort.
**Why:** FBG is the A race, 27 days out. Losing 5 days of stimulus to taper for a B+ race isn't a good trade. Coming off a strong consistent week — better to keep building.
**Stretch goal still on the table:** Sub-3:00 if you land in the right group. You're fit enough.

### Pacing
- Target NP: ~165–175W (tempo to low sweet spot off 220W FTP, IF 0.75–0.80)
- This is a flat/rolling race — find a fast group and sit in. Drafting > everything.
- Don't lead early. Save matches for the back half — same rule as Black Fork.
- Your Thursday mixed-surface ride was NP 170W for 73 min at HR 127 — that's the target zone. Hold that for 3 hrs.

### Fueling
- Normal breakfast 2–3 hrs before start (no special preload Friday)
- First gel at 20 min
- **70g carbs/hr minimum** — this is where Red Eagle still functions as a useful test. Practice for FBG.
- Products: SiS gels + Tailwind. Same as Black Fork but higher rate.
- Hydration: weather-dependent. Forecast check Saturday.

### Key difference from Black Fork
Black Fork was a climbing survival race. Red Eagle is a **speed race on flat-ish gravel.** Drafting matters more. Group selection matters more. Pacing is less about climbs and more about sustained effort. Think tempo + surges, not repeated VO2 efforts.
