### Strength program — 3 sessions/week (Mon push, Fri pull, Sat full-body)

Distributing strength so arms get real frequency without one giant session. All home dumbbells (20–40 lb range). Bump weight when form allows.

#### Mon — Upper Push + Arms (~20 min)

| Movement | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Push-ups | 3 × 10–15 | Elevate hands on a counter if shoulders are stiff post-ride. |
| DB shoulder press (seated or standing) | 3 × 10 | 20–30 lb. Don't arch the back. |
| DB curl | 3 × 10 each arm | 20–30 lb. Slow eccentric. |
| Overhead tricep extension (single DB, two hands) | 3 × 10 | 25–35 lb. Elbows by ears. |
| Lateral raise | 2 × 12 | 10–15 lb. Light, strict form. |

#### Fri — Upper Pull + Arms (~20 min)

| Movement | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Single-arm bent-over row | 3 × 10 each arm | 30–40 lb. Brace on chair/bench. |
| Reverse flye (DB, bent over) | 3 × 12 | 10–20 lb. Squeeze rear delts — anti-rounding for the bars. |
| Hammer curl | 3 × 10 each arm | 25–35 lb. |
| Tricep dips (chair) | 3 × 10 | Bodyweight. Don't let shoulders shrug. |
| Plank-to-shoulder-tap | 2 × 20 taps | Anti-rotation finisher. |

#### Sat — Full Body, Lower-Focused (~35 min)

| Movement | Sets × Reps | Notes |

| Goblet squat | 3 × 8 | Hold one DB at chest. Drive through heels. |
| DB Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 | DB in each hand. Hinge, soft knees, hamstring stretch. |

| DB floor press | 3 × 10 | Chest + tricep. Anti-droop on the bars. |

**Rule of thumb:** if Sunday's ride feels heavy from Sat strength, dial back to 2 sets/movement next week. If arms feel cooked Tue from Mon, drop a set off Mon. Strength is durable infrastructure, not Sunday-killing fatigue.
