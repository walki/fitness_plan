### Left-side rehab integration (added May 23)

Context: Left knee = 2007 ACL/MCL/meniscus blowup with persistent quad/VMO weakness. Left elbow = January 2026 overuse, not fully recovered. Roger has ~3 months of weight-training history; tendons/connective tissue still adapting. Goal: build capacity over 6-8 weeks, not chase symmetry by adding reps.

**Slot into existing sessions:**

- **Mon (Push):** Add **TKEs (terminal knee extensions) 3×15 each leg** as warm-up. Resistance band behind knee, push to lockout, hold 1s. Directly trains VMO.
- **Fri (Pull):**
  - Replace single-arm bent-over rows → **chest-supported DB rows 3×10** (face down on bench)
  - Replace hammer curls → **slow eccentric hammer curls** (3s lower / 1s up): **left 4×8 @ 10-15 lb, right 3×8 @ same weight**
- **Sat (Full body):**
  - Add **isometric Spanish squat 3×30s** as first movement (wall sit + band around shins pushing outward)
  - Replace split squats → **box step-ups 3×8 each leg, matched weight** (start 20 lb each side; drive through working heel, don't push off back foot)
- **Daily option (2 min):** isometric wrist hold @ 5-10 lb DB, arm extended, 3×30s. Palm up or palm down based on where elbow pain sits.

**Reintroduce in ~3-4 weeks if going well:** tempo split squats (4s down / 1s up), bilateral matched loading.

**Hard rule on chronic tendinopathy work (elbow):** Pain <4/10 during loading is OK. Pain that spikes higher OR lingers >24h = back off the load.

**Decision gate:** If by week 6 there's no measurable change on the left knee or elbow, see a sports PT. Hands-on work may be needed to unlock specific deficits.
