## 🏁 FBG RACE DAY PLAN — Sat Jun 13

### Pre-race morning

- Big breakfast **2-3 hrs before start**: 4 eggs + 2 toast + butter + honey + banana = ~600 cal / 80g+ carbs
- Coffee yes; no new foods
- **1 SiS gel 10-15 min before gun**
- Apply chamois cream
- Hit the bathroom early

### Pacing — by thirds

| Miles | Phase | NP target | HR ceiling | Notes |
|-------|-------|-----------|------------|-------|
| **0-22** | **DISCIPLINE** | 165-175W (IF 0.72-0.76) | **140** | Resist opening surge. Let people pass you. Your race starts at mile 22. |
| **22-44** | **RHYTHM** | 175-185W (IF 0.76-0.80) | 145 | Settle in. Climbs by RPE not power. Save matches — 15 climbs total. |
| **44-66** | **COMPETE** | 180-195W (IF 0.78-0.85) | go | If you've been disciplined, legs are here when others aren't. Last 10 mi: empty the tank. |

### Fueling — non-negotiable

- **80-90 g carbs/hr × 5 hrs = 400-450g total carbs**
- **Carry on body:** 6 SiS gels + 4-5 Tailwind stickpacks (100g+ Tailwind carbs)
- First gel **20 min** into race, then every 25-30 min (watch alarm)
- Tailwind in every bottle (50g per bottle = 2 stickpacks per fresh fill), drink continuously
- **Aid stations: water + bananas only assumption.** Don't depend on anything else.

### Hydration + sodium

- ~1 bottle/hr minimum (more if hot)
- ~700-900 mg/hr sodium from on-bike products (gels ~70mg each + Tailwind ~310mg per scoop)
- Mortal Hydration stick mid-race OK as electrolyte top-up
- **Weather check Wed/Thu** — adjust fluid plan if forecast 85°F+ or humid

### Equipment checklist (Friday)

- [ ] Grail prepped — drivetrain clean, tires checked, tubeless sealant fresh
- [ ] Power meter battery: check
- [ ] HR strap battery: check (May 25 sensor dropout during climb = warning sign)
- [ ] Garmin firmware: check for update
- [ ] **6 SiS gels in jersey pockets**
- [ ] **4-5 Tailwind stickpacks in jersey pockets**
- [ ] 2 bottles pre-mixed with Tailwind (50g per bottle)
- [ ] Spare tube + CO2 + multi-tool
- [ ] Chamois cream
- [ ] Helmet, shoes, kit, sunglasses
- [ ] Cash for parking / post-race food
- [ ] Phone in jersey pocket (zip)

### Mental approach

**Mantra:** *Don't burn matches early. Spend them when it matters.*

- Early: stay disciplined, conservation IS the strategy
- Middle: stay steady, eat and drink on schedule, notice climbs without fearing them
- Late: compete — anyone who burned matches early is hurting now, you're not
