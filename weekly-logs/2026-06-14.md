- **80-90 g carbs/hr × 5 hrs = 400-450g total carbs**

- ~700-900 mg/hr sodium from on-bike products (gels ~70mg each + Tailwind ~310mg per scoop)
- Mortal Hydration stick mid-race OK as electrolyte top-up
- **Weather check Wed/Thu** — adjust fluid plan if forecast 85°F+ or humid
