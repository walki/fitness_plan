### Weekend ride detail — 3-day block (Fri around Columbus → Sat to Yellow Springs → Sun back)

**Revised Thu Jul 2:** Sunday run is OUT — it's 3 straight ride days (maybe 4 with Monday). Fri = Columbus loop, Sat = Columbus→Yellow Springs (~60 mi), Sun = Yellow Springs→Columbus (~60 mi). Light gear (<10 lb).

**HEAT IS THE STORY. Extreme heat all weekend — the ride is steady + slow, NOT a push. Roger's plan (correct): "no need to heat stroke."**

- **Ride by HR/RPE, not power.** In this heat, HR runs high at low power (cardiac drift). If HR climbs at an easy effort, that's the heat — back off, don't chase watts. Keep it conversational.
- **Hydrate aggressively, ahead of thirst.** Tuesday's club ride hit 100°F; expect similar or worse. Last week's efforts logged ~2L+ sweat. Electrolytes matter as much as water — keep Tailwind/SiS sodium flowing so you don't cramp or hyponatremia yourself.
- **Fuel across all 3 days.** ~50-60 g carbs/hr on the bike. Recovery meals each night (protein + carbs — and get the protein back up, it's been low all week). Don't bonk day 1 and compound it over 3 days.
- **Heat illness watch:** stop if you get chills, goosebumps, stop sweating, dizzy, or nauseous. Those are not "push through" signals. Shade + fluids + cool down.
- **Don't hold a deficit.** These are 2,600-3,000+ cal days. Fuel and recover; the weight trend resumes next week.
- **This is still great training** — 3 days of aerobic volume + serious heat adaptation banked for a late-July race. Just earned slowly, not heroically.
