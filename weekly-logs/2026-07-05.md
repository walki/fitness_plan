### Weekend ride detail (Columbus ↔ Yellow Springs)

**~60 mi each way, mostly flat, light gear (<10 lb — extra kit + casual clothes, NOT loaded bikepacking).** ~3.5-4 hrs each at Z2 (HR 120-134, 129-172W). This is two big aerobic days back-to-back — the real training value of the week.

- **Fuel the rides:** ~50-70 g carbs/hr (Tailwind + SiS — lighter than race intensity since it's flat Z2, but back-to-back means don't run empty). Don't bonk day 1 and pay for it day 2.
- **Hydrate hard:** July heat, 2 long days. Last Sunday's 11-miler logged ~2L sweat loss in similar temps — drink to thirst plus a bit.
- **Friday night recovery:** protein + carbs at the Airbnb. Real meal. This sets up Saturday's ride and Sunday's run.
- **Don't hold a deficit Fri/Sat.** These are 2,600-3,000+ cal days. Eat to fuel and recover; the trend resumes next week.

### Sun run detail (on tired legs)

After ~120 mi of riding, the legs will be glycogen-depleted and fatigued. **That's the point** — BR100 Leg 8 is the anchor leg, run on a body that's already worked. Practicing running on fatigue is gold.

- **7-9 mi by feel, HR <145.** If legs are genuinely cooked, 5-6 mi easy is a win — no ego.
- Pace will be slow and that's expected. HR cap is the constraint.
- Light fuel before (coffee + something small) + Tailwind/gel if it goes past ~70 min.
