### Sun long trail run detail (BR100 build week 2 — CVNP)

**7 mi at conversational pace, HR <145.** Step up from last week's 6 mi (+1 mi distance). The big change is **elevation** — CVNP has real climbs. Pick a route with **500+ ft of gain**. This re-introduces sustained climbing under fatigue, which is the BR100 Leg 8 demand (big climb at mile 7-8 of the leg).

**Fueling:** 1 Tailwind bottle (~25g carbs/scoop) + 1-2 SiS gels mid-run. Should be ~1:30-1:40 of running.

**Pacing note:** Don't chase pace on trail. The HR cap is the constraint — if you find yourself at HR 150 on a climb, walk a step or two and let it settle. Trail running pace is whatever HR-controlled pace gives you. Last week was 12:53/mi at HR 138 with 329 ft. This week with more elevation, expect 13:00-13:30/mi pace at the same HR — that's normal.
