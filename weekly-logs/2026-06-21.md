### Sun long trail run detail (BR100 build week 1)

**6 mi at conversational pace (HR <145 ideally).** Modest step up from today's 5-mile shakeout. Pick a trail with **some elevation** to start re-introducing climbing fatigue to the legs — BR100 Leg 8 has its big climb at mile 7-8, so we ramp the climbing tolerance now. Trail running is its own skill — uneven footing, slower turnover, eccentric quad loading on descents. Don't compare pace to road runs.

**Fueling:** 1 gel mid-run or a Tailwind bottle is plenty. This is short enough you don't need a real strategy.
