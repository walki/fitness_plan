### Tuesday 4×10 Sweet Spot (`zwift-workouts/4x10.zwo`)

**FTP: 217W (stale — real FTP likely 235–250W based on Week 2 + Week 3 data)**

**Note:** Zwift rounds power targets to 5W increments. Both weeks' "sweet spot" intervals were actually executed at 185W (not 184W or 187W as prescribed). Week 2 3×10 and Week 3 4×10 were identical intensity — the improvement is purely the extra rep at lower HR.

```
Warmup:        10:00  —  120W → 160W ramp
Activation 1:   1:00  —  175W (81% FTP)
Recovery:        1:00  —  130W
Activation 2:   1:00  —  190W (88% FTP)
Recovery:        2:00  —  130W

Interval 1:    10:00  —  187W (86% FTP). Cadence 90.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W
Interval 2:    10:00  —  187W. If rep 1 felt easy, nudge to 190W.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W
Interval 3:    10:00  —  187W.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W
Interval 4:    10:00  —  187–195W. Last one. Push if you've got it.
Recovery:        5:00  —  130W

Cooldown:        5:00  —  140W → 100W
```

**Total time: ~75 min. Time at sweet spot: 40 min.**

**Execution notes:**
- This is a step up from last week's 3×10 at 184W. One more rep, slightly higher target.
- Last week HR was 135→148 (rep 1) and 147→155 (rep 3). Expect similar or slightly higher — 4th rep will push HR.
- If rep 2 feels rough, drop to 182–184W and finish all 4. Completing 4×10 > blowing up at 195W after 3×8.

### Thursday 5×3 VO2 Max (`zwift-workouts/5x3-vo2.zwo`)

```
Warmup:        10:00  —  120W → 163W ramp
Activation 1:   2:00  —  178W (82% FTP)
Recovery:        1:00  —  119W
Activation 2:   1:00  —  217W (100% FTP) — feel what threshold is
Recovery:        2:00  —  119W

Interval 1:     3:00  —  243W (112% FTP). Cadence 95–100.
Recovery:        3:00  —  119W
Interval 2:     3:00  —  243W
Recovery:        3:00  —  119W
Interval 3:     3:00  —  243W
Recovery:        3:00  —  119W
Interval 4:     3:00  —  243W
Recovery:        3:00  —  119W
Interval 5:     3:00  —  243W
Recovery:        3:00  —  119W

Cooldown:        5:00  —  140W → 98W
```

**Total time: ~51 min. Time at VO2: 15 min.**

**Execution notes (pre-ride):**
- This is a different kind of suffering than sweet spot. RPE will hit 8–9 on the last 2 reps. Your legs will scream, your lungs will burn. That's the point.
- Cadence 95–100 — keep spinning, don't grind. The power comes from turnover, not torque.
- If 243W feels impossible after rep 2, drop to 230W and finish all 5. Completing the set > hitting the number.
- Given stale FTP: 243W at real FTP of ~240W = ~101% FTP. This might actually feel more like threshold than VO2. If it feels too easy through rep 3, you have your answer about FTP.

**Post-ride results:**
- Duration: 51:11 | NP: 184W | Avg: 159W | kJ: 490 | Avg HR: 124 | **Max HR: 164** (highest of the build)
- Reps completed at 243W: 4 of 5. Rep 5 dropped to 210W (85% target) to finish the set.
- HR progression per rep: ~145 → ~155 → ~160 → ~163 → ~150 (at lower power)
- HR recovery floor rose each rep: 130 → 135 → 140 → barely recovered by rep 4
- RPE: 8–9 on reps 3–4. Felt the wall at rep 4.
- Training Effect: Tempo 3.4 — Garmin labeled it tempo not VO2 (only 12 min at target + interval power closer to threshold than pure VO2)

**Key takeaway:** Sweet spot work says FTP is 235–250W. VO2 work found the ceiling — lactate clearance / top-end recovery is the weakness. Aerobic base is deep and capable; anaerobic / VO2 system is underdeveloped from months of aerobic-focused work.

**Implications:**
- For Black Fork (punchy 1–3 min climbs with natural descent recovery): **not a concern.** Different demand than ERG-mode VO2 with fixed 3-min rest. You have the aerobic capacity to handle it.
- For FBG and future threshold/VO2 development: **continue this work after Black Fork.** 1×/week VO2 during the Red Eagle → FBG block will meaningfully move this ceiling.
- For FTP testing: the sweet spot data is more representative of sustainable power than the VO2 ceiling. Post-Florida 20-min test should give a real number.
