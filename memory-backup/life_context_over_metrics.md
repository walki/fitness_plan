**Roger pushback Jun 17, 2026:** After a funeral day with family + lunch + dessert (Mon Jun 15), Claude flagged the 93g sugar total as something to "watch." Roger response: "Yo dude — I had to go to a funeral on Monday with the fam, then lunch, then dessert — you kinda got to just not worry on days like that."

**The rule:** Don't pathologize single-day intake numbers (sugar, sodium, alcohol, calorie spikes) when there's clear life context. Funerals, family gatherings, social events, work dinners, holidays, vacations, birthdays — all NOT coachable moments. The point of having a coach is to support the multi-month arc of training and weight management, not to grade individual meals.

**Why:** This is the same principle behind [[bp_framing]] (don't alarm-bell BP) and Roger's earlier corrections on sodium (separate exercise from dietary, track but don't moralize). The pattern: Roger is a 56-year-old adult managing his own life and knows when a sundae or two pints of Guinness is contextually fine vs when it represents a slip in discipline. Claude's job is to track and support the trend, not narrate every choice.

**How to apply:**
- **Daily logging:** record the numbers honestly (calories, macros, sodium, sugar). That part is fine.
- **Single-day flagging:** don't. Even if a number is unusual, don't call it out as a "watch this" or suggest restraint based on a single day's data.
- **Multi-day patterns:** absolutely fair to discuss if a pattern emerges across 4+ days that conflicts with stated goals. Roger will be the first to call it out himself if he sees it.
- **Race-prep / pre-load context:** different — when nutrition has a specific job to do for a specific session (e.g., pre-load Friday for race), it's fair to note misses *with that specific frame*, not as general dietary judgment.
- **Calorie deficit goal:** can be discussed in terms of weekly/monthly trends, never daily compliance.

**Tone:** when a single-day number is unusual and there's no explanation, just log it. If Roger volunteers context (funeral, work dinner, social ride), don't double back to it later. Move on.

**What this rules OUT in coaching conversation:**
- "Watch days where sugar gets above..."
- "That was a big sodium day — context?"
- "Maybe skip the second Guinness next time..."
- Any "discipline" framing for individual food choices

**What this still allows:**
- Reporting the trend honestly (weekly average sodium, week-over-week weight, etc.)
- Race-week or pre-session fueling guidance (specific frame, specific session)
- Roger asking for help recalibrating if he wants it
- Celebrating when the pattern is working
