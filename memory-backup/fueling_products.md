Roger's go-to endurance fueling products, validated by extensive experimentation:

- **SiS (Science in Sport) gels** — primary gel of choice
- **Tailwind** — primary drink mix
- **Skratch chews** — occasional supplement (not core)
- **Mortal Hydration** — electrolyte stick, used mid-race for sodium top-up (note: not in MFP; ~250mg sodium per stick)

**Why these:** He's tried many gels and drink mixes over the years. These two are his go-tos "by a lot" for both **taste** and **GI tolerance**. He can knock back SiS + Tailwind at high rates with no negative effects.

**Validated capacity:** ~106 g carbs/hr in 82°F heat at race intensity (Red Eagle May 17, 2026) — no GI distress. Used for FBG fueling planning.

**Coaching implication:** Don't propose new brands or experiment with substitutes when planning races. Fueling protocols should be built around SiS + Tailwind as the assumed delivery system. Higher carb rates can be planned with confidence using these specifically.

**Sodium framing — important:** Roger has ultra-running background and understands sodium loss during exercise better than typical endurance athletes. **Separate exercise sodium from dietary sodium** when discussing his intake. Exercise sodium (gels, drink mix, electrolyte sticks during a ride) is getting consumed in sweat and isn't BP-relevant — typical sweat loss for him is ~1,000 mg/L × sweat volume. Standard sports recommendation 500-1,000 mg/hr; he can go higher in heat. **The BP-relevant sodium is the DIETARY baseline (off-bike food).** Don't conflate the two. When reporting sodium, separate exercise intake from dietary intake so the chronic-load picture is honest.
