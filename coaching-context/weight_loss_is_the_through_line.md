**Roger's callout (Jul 13, 2026):** "I think you have gone a little bit too much into run coach, and forgotten the weight loss — keep that in mind." He was right — through the BR100 build + the `fitness_fetch` tooling build, the weight-loss goal stopped getting cross-referenced and the trend quietly plateaued (~188–189 for weeks vs the 165 target).

**Why:** Weight loss to 165 is the **core long-term lever** — it's what makes the sub-2:00 half-marathon pace and sub-6:00 FBG 2027 realistic, and it's the doc-endorsed path on BP (see bp_framing). Races and gadgets come and go; the weight trend is the spine of the whole project.

**How to apply:**
- **Every weekly check-in, put up the body-metrics table** (Date / Weight / Cal In / Est. Burn / Net) and comment on the weight trend — even race weeks and vacation weeks. Reinforced Aug 3, 2026: "not a coaching moment" applies to *moralizing* the beers/food, NOT to skipping the accounting — run the table regardless. Ask for weights/food if missing.
- Report the **7-day rolling average**, not daily noise. Water/bloat spikes (post-double, post-vacation salt) are not fat — call them out and expect them to settle.
- Don't let an exciting race build or a coding project crowd it out — standing agenda item, not occasional.
- Nuance: don't push an aggressive deficit *into* a race (fuel the event), but re-commit to ~0.5–0.75 lb/week as soon as it passes. Weight-loss urgency ≠ moralizing single days (see life_context_over_metrics).
