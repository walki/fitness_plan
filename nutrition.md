## 🥚 Protein Priority — The #1 Focus

**Target: 120g/day minimum to start. Build to 150g.**

### What 120g looks like in a day:

| Meal | Food | Protein |
|------|------|---------|
| Breakfast | 3 eggs + 1 cup cottage cheese | ~43g |
| Lunch | 5 oz chicken breast or canned tuna | ~35g |
| Dinner | 5 oz meat or fish | ~35g |
| **Total** | | **~113g** |

Already within reach of 120g before any snacks. The kitchen scale makes this accurate.

**High-protein anchors already in rotation:**
- Cottage cheese (1 cup = ~25g) ✅ already doing this
- Eggs (3 = ~18g)
- Greek yogurt (1 cup = ~17–20g)
- Chicken breast (4 oz = ~35g)
- Canned tuna (1 can = ~25g)
- Protein shake (~25g) — useful as a bridge, not a crutch

## 🕐 Meal Timing

**The core problem:** Skipping breakfast → under-eating through the day → arriving at dinner ravenous → large dinner + evening snacks + alcohol.

**The fix:** Eat breakfast with protein within 1 hour of waking. This single change cascades positively through the whole day.

### Training day vs rest day approach:

**Training days (rides, runs):**
- Pre-workout: real breakfast 2–3 hrs before, or small carb snack 45–60 min before
- During: fuel the work — 60–70g carbs/hr on rides over 90 min
- Post-workout: protein within 45 min of finishing
- Deficit comes from the rest of the day, not from skimping on workout fuel

**Rest days:**
- Same protein target
- Slightly lower carbs naturally — no need to force it
- Good day to be more mindful of total calories

## 🍺 Alcohol

**Current:** 10–15 drinks/week (fluctuates)
**Calorie impact:** ~1,000–2,000 kcal/week untracked

This is likely the single biggest invisible calorie source. Not asking to stop — asking to count it. Log it in MFP like anything else.

On race weeks and key build weeks: worth actively reducing. Not for moral reasons — just because alcohol disrupts sleep quality and recovery, which undermines the training you just did.

## ✈️ Travel Protocol

Travel weeks will always be harder. The goal is not perfection — it's not losing the thread entirely.

- Photo everything you eat, log later
- Prioritize protein at every meal even when choices are limited
- Accept that tracking will be rougher — don't use it as an excuse to stop entirely
- Check in with coach more frequently on travel weeks, not less

## 🚫 What We're NOT Doing

- No crash dieting
- No skipping fuel on long rides to "save calories"
- No aggressive restriction on race weeks

- Not overhauling everything at once — one habit at a time
