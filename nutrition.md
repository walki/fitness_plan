## What's Working

- **Whey protein powder** — single biggest intervention. One scoop (25–33g protein) closes the daily gap. When the habit is on, protein hits 150g+. When it breaks (FL trip, social weeks), protein drops to 130s.
- **Pre-race fueling protocol** — 2,500+ cal / 300g+ carbs the day before. Validated twice (Sunday long ride Apr 12, Black Fork May 2). Night and day difference vs Mo Gritty bonk.
- **Daily MFP tracking** — now a habit. Imperfect but consistent.
- **High-protein anchors:** cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, eggs, chicken breast, salmon, tuna. These are the foundation.
- **Breakfast protein matters** — days starting with yogurt/eggs land 140g+. Coffee-only breakfasts consistently miss the target.

## What's Not Working Yet

- **On-bike carb rate:** Still averaging 42–58g/hr vs 70g target. Not a fueling product problem (SiS, Tailwind, Hammer are all good) — it's a volume/timing problem. Need to practice at 70g/hr outside of races.
- **Protein consistency across the week:** Hits 150g+ for 3–4 days, then slips to 130s. The whey habit breaks during disruptions (travel, social weeks) and takes days to restart.
- **Beer calories:** Variable week to week. Some weeks minimal, some weeks 1,800+ cal. Always tracked now, but still a significant calorie source that displaces protein-rich food.

## Protein — 150g Daily

**Target: 150g/day. Non-negotiable.**

### What 150g looks like in a day:

| Meal | Example | Protein |
|------|---------|---------|
| Breakfast | Greek yogurt + honey + coffee w/ milk | ~15–25g |
| Lunch | Chicken breast + bread + cottage cheese | ~45–55g |
| Dinner | Eggs or salmon or chicken + veg + grain | ~35–45g |
| Evening | **Whey shake with milk** | **25–33g** |
| **Total** | | **~130–158g** |

**The whey shake is the closer.** Without it, most days land at 120–130g. With it, 150g+. This is the single habit that makes or breaks the protein target.

### Rules:
1. Don't let breakfast be just coffee — at minimum, add a yogurt (11g)
2. Whey shake every evening — non-negotiable
3. On double-whey days (56g powder = 33g protein), the gap closes even faster
4. Pizza MFP entries often show 0g protein — they're wrong. Don't rely on them.

## On-Bike Fueling

**Target: 70g carbs/hr from minute 20. For any ride over 90 min.**

### Current products:
- SiS isotonic gels (22g carbs each)
- Tailwind Endurance Fuel (25g carbs per scoop)
- Hammer Gel (21g carbs per packet)
- Mortal Hydration (8g carbs)
- Skratch Hyper Hydration (sodium loading)
- Coca-Cola (for race day — caffeine + sugar)

### To hit 70g/hr:
- **1 SiS gel every 20 min** (3/hr × 22g = 66g) + Tailwind in bottle = 70–80g/hr
- OR **2 SiS gels/hr + 1 scoop Tailwind** = 69g/hr
- Start eating at **minute 20**. Not minute 40. Not when hungry.

### Race-day fueling comparison:

| Race | On-bike carbs/hr | Pre-load | Result |
|------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| Mo Gritty | 58g/hr | 1,756 cal (bad) | Bonked at mile 40, napped |
| Black Fork | 42g/hr | Good | Finished strong (cold weather helped) |
| Target for FBG | **70g/hr** | 2,500+ cal | — |

## Alcohol

**Current state:** Being tracked in MFP. Variable — some weeks minimal, some weeks 1,800+ cal.

**Impact:** Beer displaces protein-rich food, disrupts sleep quality, and adds significant empty calories. A 72oz IPA session = 900 cal = nearly a full meal's worth of calories with zero protein.

**Approach:** Not asking to stop. Asking to:
1. Keep counting it (already happening)
2. Minimize on race weeks and key build weeks
3. Recognize when a heavy beer week pushes protein below target and calories above

## Health Context

Roger is on blood pressure medication. Doctor says weight loss is part of the treatment plan. Nutrition isn't just about performance — it's about long-term health.

- Weight loss directly improves BP
- Protein supports muscle mass during deficit (protects metabolism)
- Consistent fueling prevents the binge-restrict cycle that stalls progress
- Tracking BP alongside weight gives real data for doctor visits
