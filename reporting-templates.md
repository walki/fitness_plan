# 🧠 INTERPRETATION RULES

## Weight Trends
- Use 7-day average ONLY
- Ignore daily fluctuations
- Look for:
  - ~0.5–0.75 lb/week loss

---

## Performance Signals
- Sweet spot should feel controlled (RPE 4–6)
- Z2 HR stable over time
- Long ride fatigue manageable

---

## Warning Signs
- HR unusually high at normal effort
- Persistent fatigue
- Sleep disruption
- Weight dropping too fast (>1 lb/week consistently)

---

# 🔁 FEEDBACK LOOP

1. Log workouts + weight
2. Submit weekly summary
3. Adjust:
   - calories
   - training load
   - recovery
