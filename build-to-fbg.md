# 🏁 Build Plan → Funk Bottoms Gravel (200k+)

## 🎯 Objective
Arrive with:
- High durability (6–10 hr effort)
- Strong aerobic base
- Ability to handle repeated rollers
- Lower body weight

---

## 🗓 Timeline

### Weeks 1–3 (Now → Black Fork)
Focus:
- Rebuild rhythm
- Sweet spot progression
- Weight consistency

Bike:
- 3×10 → 3×12

Long Ride:
- 2.5–3 hrs

Run:
- 2×/week steady

---

### Weeks 4–5 (Black Fork → Red Eagle)
Focus:
- Increase intensity tolerance
- Practice race pacing

Bike:
- Introduce:
  - 2×20
  - Over/unders

Long Ride:
- 3–3.5 hrs

---

### Weeks 6–8 (Red Eagle → Funk Bottoms)
Focus:
- Maximum durability
- Rolling terrain fatigue resistance

Bike:
- Rolling efforts:
  - 10–15 min segments slightly above Z2
- Maintain sweet spot

Long Ride:
- 3–4+ hrs
- Include terrain simulation

---

## 🚴 Race Strategy Practice

### Black Fork
- Race effort
- Push earlier than Mid South
- Test higher effort on rollers

---

### Red Eagle
- Hard but controlled
- Practice pacing discipline

---

### Funk Bottoms (A Race)
- Early discipline
- Fuel consistently
- Ride steady until final 25%

---

## 🍌 Fueling Targets

- 70–80g carbs/hr
- Start early (within 20 min)
- No missed feeds
- Practice every long ride

---

## ⚠️ Key Risks

- Underfueling long rides
- Going too hard too early in races
- Not simulating rolling terrain fatigue
- Losing strength during weight loss

---

## 📈 Success Indicators

- Long rides feel controlled
- HR stable at endurance power
- Ability to surge late in rides
- No bonking
- Gradual weight drop

---

## 🧠 Mental Strategy

- Early: stay disciplined
- Middle: stay steady
- Late: compete

Mantra:
"Don't burn matches early. Spend them when it matters."
