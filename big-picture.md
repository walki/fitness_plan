# 🧭 12-Month Training & Health Plan

## 🎯 Primary Goals
- Weight: 194 → 165 lbs
- FTP: ~210 → ~240–250W
- Running: Progress toward sub-2:00 half marathon
- Maintain consistency, durability, and health

---

## 🧱 Guiding Principles
- Consistency > intensity
- Weight loss is a force multiplier
- Never sacrifice recovery for short-term gains
- Train like an athlete, not like you're dieting
- Build in phases, not randomly

---

## 📆 Annual Phases

### 🟢 Phase 1: Rebuild + Weight Momentum (Now → June)
- Focus:
  - Resume structure post Mid South
  - Establish caloric deficit (~0.5–0.75 lb/week)
  - Build sweet spot capacity
  - Maintain 2 runs/week
- Key events:
  - Black Fork
  - Red Eagle
  - Funk Bottoms (A race)

---

### 🟡 Phase 2: FTP + Volume Build (Summer)
- Increase interval duration (2×20, longer tempo)
- Longer endurance rides (3–5 hrs)
- Maintain weight loss or stabilize near goal
- Maintain strength training

---

### 🔵 Phase 3: Run Focus + Performance (Fall)
- Increase run frequency and duration
- Introduce tempo runs and progression runs
- Maintain cycling base
- Target half marathon performance

---

### ⚫ Phase 4: Base + Strength Reset (Winter)
- Lower intensity cycling
- Strength progression focus
- Maintain weight
- Prepare for next season

---

## ⚖️ Weight Strategy

- Target: ~0.5–0.75 lb/week
- Avg intake: ~2300–2400 kcal
- Protein: 150g+ daily
- Carbs aligned with training days
- No aggressive dieting during race weeks

---

## 📊 Weekly Check-In (Every Sunday)

- Avg weight (7-day)
- Avg calorie intake
- Avg calorie burn (Garmin)
- Training summary
- Sleep / mood (1–5)

Adjust based on trends, not daily noise.

---

## 🧠 Success Metrics

- Consistent training weeks
- Stable HR at given outputs
- Gradual weight reduction
- Improved durability (long ride feel)
- Improved run efficiency

---

## 🚫 Avoid

- Crash dieting
- Skipping fueling during long rides
- Over-testing FTP
- Chasing fatigue
