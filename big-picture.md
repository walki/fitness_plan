## Guiding Principles

- Consistency > intensity
- Weight loss is a force multiplier — for performance AND health
- Never sacrifice recovery for short-term gains
- Train like an athlete, not like you're dieting
- Build in phases, not randomly
- Pre-load before big efforts. Fuel the work.
- Ride by HR — it's been the most reliable guide

## Weight Strategy

- **Ultimate target:** 165 lbs
- **Rate:** 0.5–0.75 lb/week. As long as trending downward, we're good.
- **Current:** 188.5 lbs (May 10). Down 5.5 lbs from 194 start.
- **Calorie approach:** Tiered by day type
  - Rest/walk day: 2,100–2,200 cal
  - Light training day: 2,400–2,500 cal
  - Hard/long training day: 2,700–2,800 cal
  - Event/race day: match the burn — no deficit
  - Pre-race day: 2,500+ cal / 300g+ carbs
- Protein: 150g/day (whey protein powder is the key lever)
- Carbs aligned with training days
- No aggressive dieting during race weeks
- **On-bike fueling:** Target 70g carbs/hr. Currently averaging 42–58g/hr — critical gap for longer events.

- Resting HR (weekly avg from watch)
- Blood pressure (2×/week — Monday and Friday mornings, Index BPM)
