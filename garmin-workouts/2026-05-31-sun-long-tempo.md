# Garmin Workout — Sun May 31 Long Tempo Ride

> **3 hours. 4 x 20-min tempo blocks separated by 20-min Z2 recovery.**
> HR-targeted (outdoor on Grail). Power is the secondary indicator, HR drives execution.
> Build this manually in Garmin Connect, then send to your device.

---

## Step-by-step build (Garmin Connect web)

1. Go to **connect.garmin.com** → sign in
2. Top nav: **Training & Planning → Workouts**
3. Click **Create a Workout** → **Cycling**
4. Name it: **`Sun Long Tempo 3hr`**
5. Click **Add Step** for each of the 9 steps below — use **Heart Rate → Custom Heart Rate** as the target for every step
6. After all 9 steps are in, click **Save**
7. From the workout detail page, click **Send to Device** (or schedule it on May 31 in the calendar to push automatically next sync)

---

## The 9 steps

| # | Step Name | Type | Duration | HR Target | Notes |
|---|-----------|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | **Warmup Z2** | Warm Up | 20:00 | **100 – 130 bpm** | Easy spin, let HR settle into Z2 |
| 2 | **Tempo 1** | Active | 20:00 | **135 – 145 bpm** | First tempo block — find the rhythm |
| 3 | **Z2 Recovery 1** | Rest | 20:00 | **115 – 130 bpm** | Real recovery, not drifting back to tempo |
| 4 | **Tempo 2** | Active | 20:00 | **135 – 145 bpm** | Should still feel sustainable |
| 5 | **Z2 Recovery 2** | Rest | 20:00 | **115 – 130 bpm** | Eat. Drink. Reset. |
| 6 | **Tempo 3** | Active | 20:00 | **135 – 145 bpm** | Halfway through — pace check |
| 7 | **Z2 Recovery 3** | Rest | 20:00 | **115 – 130 bpm** | Last recovery before the final push |
| 8 | **Tempo 4 (Push)** | Active | 20:00 | **135 – 150 bpm** | Final tempo — push if legs feel it |
| 9 | **Cool Down** | Cool Down | 20:00 | **100 – 125 bpm** | Easy spin home |

**Total: 9 steps, 3 hours.**

**Step Type field meaning in Garmin Connect:**
- *Warm Up* = first step convention; lap auto-starts on advance
- *Active* = work step
- *Rest* = recovery step
- *Cool Down* = last step convention

(These labels just affect how the device organizes laps and post-ride summary. The HR target is what drives execution.)

## Fueling reminder (during the ride)

**Target: 90 g carbs/hr (270g total over 3 hrs)**

- Carry: 6 SiS gels + 5.5 scoops Tailwind (in bottles or sticks)
- 2 gels/hr + ~2 scoops Tailwind/hr
- **Set a watch alarm every 30 min for a gel** — don't trust hunger
- Sip Tailwind continuously between gels

This is the FBG dress rehearsal. If gut handles 90 g/hr at tempo intensity for 3 hrs, FBG is ready.

---

## What to expect during the ride (first-time using a Garmin workout)

- Garmin will show the **current step name, target HR range, and elapsed time in step** on the workout screen
- It **vibrates/beeps when transitioning** between steps
- If your HR drifts out of range, you'll get an alert (low or high)
- You can **press Lap to skip ahead** if you need to (e.g., traffic, climb that forces effort outside the zone)
- **HR alerts can be annoying** — on a hot day with cardiac drift, you might be slightly over Z2 ceiling during recoveries. If they get noisy, you can disable HR alerts in the workout settings or just ignore them
- The cooldown step ending doesn't end the activity — you still need to **stop and save** the activity manually
