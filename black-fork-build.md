## 🌴 Apr 17–24: Florida Expedition

**This is not a training block. This is a life block.**

280 miles, 7 days, loaded bike, group pace, gators, singletrack, sand, swamp. The fitness stimulus is real but incidental — the point is having fun on bikes.

**Coaching notes for Florida:**
- Ride the pace of the group. You're the strongest rider — that means you set the tone by being calm, not by riding away.
- Day 1 legs ≠ Day 7 legs. Treat every day like you have three more after it.
- No power meter (Kona/Cutthroat). Use HR and feel. Aerobic cruise = 110–125 bpm. Don't push above 140 unless the terrain forces it.
- Eat and drink before you're hungry or thirsty. Loaded bike + heat = higher fuel demand than you expect.
- Sleep is training. Prioritize it.

**🧱 Core during Florida:** Keep the micro sessions going — 2–3× during the week, whenever convenient. Morning before riding, evening at camp, whatever. It's 5 minutes and keeps the habit alive.

**What Florida does for Black Fork:** 280 miles of aerobic work on a loaded bike primes your engine beautifully. You'll return tired but deeply fit. The recovery week converts that into race-day sharpness.

### Wed Apr 29 — FTP Validation Protocol

Current FTP is 217W (set ~Jan 2026). After Florida's aerobic volume block, it's likely shifted. Use Tuesday Apr 8's 3×10 sweet spot as a diagnostic (if 184W felt easy at low HR, FTP has moved). Then validate here:

**Option A — 20-min test:** Warm up 15 min with 2×1 min at 190W, then ride 20 min as hard as sustainable. FTP = avg power × 0.95. This gives a real number for Black Fork pacing.

**Option B — Ramp test:** If legs are too tired from Florida for a 20-min effort, do a standard ramp test instead. Less accurate but lower cost.

## 🏁 May 2: Black Fork Race Day

**Course reality:** 8 climbs of 300 ft+, punchy not sustained, chunky Ohio gravel, 72% unpaved. More neuromuscular than Mid South. You'll surge and recover repeatedly.

### Execution plan

**Miles 0–10:** Sit in. Resist the opening surge — everyone goes out too hard on gravel. Let people pass you. Your race starts at mile 15.

**Miles 10–35:** Find your rhythm. Target NP 155–165W on power sections. On climbs, effort by feel — don't bury yourself on any single climb. There are 8 of them; save matches for the later ones.

**Miles 35–54:** Now you compete. If you've been disciplined, you'll have legs here when others don't. This is where Black Fork is won or lost.

### Fueling
- Start eating within 20 min of the gun. Don't wait until hungry.
- 60–70g carbs/hr minimum
- No missed feeds — Mohican hills on empty is not fun

### Pacing anchor
Your Mid South NP was 158W for 8 hours. Black Fork should feel **noticeably harder per hour** — it's half the distance and more climbing-dense. That's expected and correct. Don't be alarmed by higher effort numbers early.
