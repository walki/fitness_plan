# 📅 Weekly Training Template

## 🧱 Weekly Anchors

- Long Ride → Sunday
- Short Intervals → Tuesday
- Long Intervals → Wed/Thu (flex)
- 2 Runs / week
- 1–2 Strength sessions
- 2–3 Core micro sessions

---

## Monday
- OFF or walk
- Optional: Core (5–10 min)

---

## Tuesday (Busy Day)
### 🚴 Short Interval Session (Trainer)
- Duration: 40–50 min
- Examples:
  - 3×10 sweet spot
  - 4×8 sweet spot
  - Over/unders (later)

---

## Wednesday OR Thursday (Key Session)
### 🚴 Long Interval Session (Trainer)
- Duration: 60–75 min
- Examples:
  - 3×12 sweet spot
  - 2×20 sweet spot
  - Rolling terrain simulation

---

## Other Day (Wed/Thu)
### 🏃 Run #1
- 45–60 min
- HR: 130–135
- Easy aerobic only

---

## Friday
### 🚴 Z2 Ride
- 60–75 min
- Power: 140–150W
- Smooth cadence

---

## Saturday
### 🟡 Light Day
Options:
- Easy spin (30–60 min)
- Run #2
- Rest

---

## Sunday (Anchor)
### 🚴 Long Ride
- 2.5–4 hrs depending on phase
- Power: steady Z2
- Fuel properly

---

## 🧱 Core (2–3× per week)
- Plank (45–60 sec)
- Side plank (30 sec each side)
- Dead bugs
- Bird dogs

---

## 💪 Strength (1–2× per week)
- Goblet squat
- RDL
- DB bench
- Rows
- Progressive overload over time

---

## ⚖️ Nutrition Integration
- Fuel workouts properly
- Maintain weekly calorie deficit
- Protein prioritized daily
