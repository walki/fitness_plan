## 🎯 What we're optimizing for

FBG is a climbing race. 6,186 ft over 66 mi = **94 ft/mi climbing density** (same as Black Fork, which Roger finished strong at NP 178W / 5:01 with stale FTP). 15 climbs means the race is won or lost in the **supra-threshold reaction** — the ability to surge above FTP repeatedly and recover at sweet spot while still riding hard. That's the gap Roger named after Red Eagle: he can sit at FTP, but blows up over it.

**Three things have to be true on race day:**
1. **Durability** — 5+ hrs of riding, last hour as strong as the first
2. **Over-FTP repeatability** — surge, recover, surge, recover, 15 times
3. **Gut tolerance at race intensity** — 80-90 g/hr at IF 0.78-0.82 for 5+ hrs

## 🏁 FBG Race Day Plan

### Pre-race
- Big breakfast 2-3 hrs before start: eggs + oatmeal + toast (500+ cal, 80g+ carbs)
- 1 SiS gel at 10-15 min before gun
- Coffee yes, no new foods
- Apply chamois cream

### Pacing — by thirds
**Miles 0-22 (first 1/3):** **Discipline phase.** Resist the opening surge. NP target: 165-175W (IF 0.72-0.76). HR ceiling 140 during this section, even on climbs. People will pass you. Your race starts at mile 22.

**Miles 22-44 (middle 1/3):** **Rhythm phase.** Settle into NP 175-185W (IF 0.76-0.80). On climbs, effort by RPE — don't bury yourself on any single climb. 15 climbs means save matches.

**Miles 44-66 (final 1/3):** **Compete phase.** If you've been disciplined, you'll have legs here when others don't. This is where FBG is won or lost. Climbs feel harder, hold form. Last 10 miles can run high — empty the tank.

### Fueling — non-negotiable
- **80-90 g carbs/hr** for ~5 hrs = 400-450g total carbs
- **Carry on body:** 6 SiS gels + 4-5 Tailwind stickpacks (100g+ Tailwind carbs to bring)
- **Aid station strategy:** assume aid stations have water and bananas only. Anything you need to race lives on you.
- First gel at 20 min into race, then every 25-30 min
- Tailwind in every bottle (mix at 50g per bottle), drink continuously
- Watch alarm for gels — don't trust hunger

### Sodium
- ~700-900 mg/hr from on-bike products is right for hot summer day
- Don't worry about exceeding "daily target" — exercise sodium gets used in sweat
- Mortal Hydration sticks fine as a mid-race electrolyte top-up

### Hydration
- ~1 bottle/hr minimum (more in heat)
- Weather check Saturday — adjust upward if hot
